KöchThe Road to Iyık Janyl · Apple II edition · 1985 · now freeware

The Iradi American Association's computer club made this edition in Chicago, with hi-res pictures of every landmark, a family of five who can fall ill and die, river crossings and hunting with the eagle.

48K · Disk II

Click the screen and use your keyboard. Press Return to stop while travelling. When hunting, J and L turn and the space bar looses the eagle. On a phone, use these keys:

Play it on an Apple II

  1. Download koch.dsk. The disk boots by itself and needs no DOS.
  2. Put it in drive 1 of an Apple II, or of AppleWin, and switch on.
  3. Loading takes about 20 seconds at normal disk speed.

To type the listing in instead, first enter POKE 103,1:POKE 104,64:POKE 16384,0:NEW so the program sits above the hi-res screen, then paste it and type RUN.
