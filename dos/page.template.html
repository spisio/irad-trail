MS-DOS edition · GW-BASIC · EGA · 1990

The Irad Trail

Spring 1877. Lead your family 690 versts by horse caravan from the Chu to Iyık Janyl. Stock up at Osmon's shop, ford the rivers, cross the high passes, hunt with your golden eagle, and raft the Naryn gorge to Cathedral Hill. The best scores go on the Top Ten. Your grave stays on the road for the next player.

Other editions: Apple II (1985 style) · Commodore 64 (1978 style)

Irad Personal Computer · EGA

Click the screen and use your keyboard. Press Enter on the road to size up the situation. Hunting: arrow keys walk and aim, Space looses your eagle. Rafting: left and right arrows steer. On a phone, use the keys below.

New in this edition

  • 16-color EGA scenesPainted landmarks, a travel strip with your caravan and a status panel.
  • Hunting with a berkutWalk the field, aim in eight directions and loose your golden eagle at hares, marmots, roe deer and arkhar. Every flight costs a meal of eagle food. Carry home 100 funt at most.
  • Rafting the NarynAt the gorge, raft to Iyık Janyl and steer around rocks, or pay the herders for the mountain road.
  • Top Ten and gravesHigh scores are saved. When a leader dies, the grave and epitaph wait by the road for the next caravan.

Play it in DOS

The game is a plain GW-BASIC program, IRAD.BAS. The Top Ten and the graves are saved next to it as IRADTOP.DAT and IRADGRV.DAT.

  1. Easiest: install PC-BASIC, a free GW-BASIC emulator, and run pcbasic IRAD.BAS.
  2. Or in DOSBox with your own copy of GWBASIC.EXE: GWBASIC IRAD.BAS.
  3. It needs an EGA or VGA card (SCREEN 7) and about 60K of free BASIC memory.
